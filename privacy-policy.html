Oort War TD - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Oort War TD is a single-player tower defence game published by Cave Age Games. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you, and the only information that ever leaves your device is your scores and achievements, sent to Google Play Games Services, and only if you choose to sign in.

Information we collect

None. We have no servers, no accounts of our own, and no database. Oort War TD has no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data, and there is nothing in it that reports back to us about how you play.

Google Play Games Services

The game offers online leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games Services, which is run by Google. This is optional. If you are signed in to Play Games on your device:

  • your score is sent to Google when a run finishes, so it can appear on the leaderboards
  • your achievement unlocks are sent to Google, so they appear on your Play Games profile
  • Google identifies you to the game by your Play Games player ID

That information is held by Google under your own Google account, not by us. We never receive it, store it or share it with anyone, and we have no way to look up an individual player. What we can see is the same thing you can: the public leaderboards.

If you are not signed in, or you decline to sign in, none of this happens and nothing leaves your device. The whole game remains playable, and nothing is withheld from you: your scores and achievements are still recorded on your own device exactly as before.

Google's handling of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What other players can see

If you appear on an online leaderboard, other players can see the name on your Google Play Games profile along with your score and your position. That name is the one you chose with Google, not the player name you type into the game, and you can change it in your Play Games profile settings. Whether you appear publicly at all is controlled by your own Play Games sharing settings, not by us.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. That includes the profile names you type in, high scores, achievements, upgrade choices, a run in progress, and your game settings. This information is not sent to us, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Device backup

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, the same way it backs up other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Deleting your data

Everything the game saves on your device is deleted when you uninstall it, and your in-game profiles can be deleted from the profile screen at any time.

Your Play Games scores and achievements are held by Google under your Google account. You can delete your Play Games data for this game, or for all games, from your Google Play Games settings or your Google account at myaccount.google.com. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing for you to request from us - but if you have a question about it, the contact address below works.

Vibration

The game asks permission to vibrate your phone during certain effects. This is used only for vibration and can be switched off in Options.

Internet access

The game asks permission to use the internet for one purpose only: Google Play Games Services, as described above. Nothing else in the game uses the network. Every sound is generated on your device as you play, and there is no content downloaded, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Google Play Games Services has its own age requirements and parental controls, which are managed through the player's Google account.

Who publishes the game

Oort War TD is published by Cave Age Games.

Changes to this policy
