Oort War TD - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Oort War TD is a single-player tower defence game published by Cave Age Games. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Oort War TD has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no internet access. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. That includes the profile names you type in, high scores, achievements, upgrade choices, a run in progress, and your game settings. This information never leaves your device through the game, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Device backup

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, the same way it backs up other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Vibration

The game asks permission to vibrate your phone during certain effects. This is used only for vibration and can be switched off in Options.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Who publishes the game

Oort War TD is published by Cave Age Games.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
