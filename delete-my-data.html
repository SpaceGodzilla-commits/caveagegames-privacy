Oort War TD - Deleting your data

Last updated: October 26, 2026

There is no account to delete and no data of yours for us to hold. Oort War TD has no servers, no database and no accounts of its own. Everything the game records about you is either kept on your own device, or held by Google under your own Google account.

That means you do not have to ask us to delete anything, and there is nothing we could delete on your behalf even if you did. Here is where each kind of data lives and how to remove it.

Your progress, scores and settings

These are stored on your device only, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling Oort War TD deletes all of it. Within the game you can also delete an individual player profile and everything recorded under it from the profile screen.

Your leaderboard scores and achievements

If you chose to sign in, these are held by Google Play Games Services under your Google account, not by us. You can remove them yourself:

  1. Open the Play Games app, or go to myaccount.google.com.
  2. Find Settings, then Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. You can delete your data for a single game - choose Oort War TD - or your whole Play Games profile. You can also do this at play.google.com/games/profile, under Your Data.

Google's own instructions are at Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile. Because this data is held under your Google account, Google is the only one who can remove it, and you do not need our permission or involvement to do so.

If you never signed in

Then nothing ever left your device, and uninstalling the game is the whole of it.

Questions, or a request

If you would like to check any of this, or you believe we hold something about you after all, write to caveagegames@protonmail.com and we will answer. Please include the name of the game. We will reply within 30 days.
